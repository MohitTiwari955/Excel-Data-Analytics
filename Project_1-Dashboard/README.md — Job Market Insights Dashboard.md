# 📊 Job Market Insights Dashboard

An interactive **Excel dashboard** for exploring job postings, salaries, companies, locations, job types, job platforms, and work-from-home opportunities across different roles.

This is my **first Data Analytics portfolio project**, built while learning Excel and dashboard development.

## 🎯 Project Overview

The dashboard allows users to select a **Job Title, Country, and Job Type** using dropdown controls and dynamically explore the job market.

It can help answer questions like:

- 💰 What is the median salary for a particular role?
- 🏢 Which companies are hiring the most?
- 🌎 Which countries have the most opportunities?
- 🏠 How many jobs offer work-from-home opportunities?
- 💼 How does job type affect the number of opportunities?
- 🔎 Which platforms have the most job postings?
- 📊 How does a selected role compare with other job titles?

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes:

- **Median Salary by Job Title**
- **Top 5 Companies Hiring**
- **Work From Home vs. Non-Work From Home**
- **Total Jobs Analyzed**
- **Top Job Platform**
- **Dynamic Job Title, Country & Job Type selection**
- **Selected Job Title highlighting** for easier salary comparison

## 💡 Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify patterns in the job market, including:

### Salary
- Compare median salaries across different job titles.
- Identify roles with relatively higher or lower median salaries.
- Compare a selected role against other roles in the dataset.

### Companies
- Identify the companies with the most job postings for a selected role.
- See how the leading employers change when different job titles or locations are selected.

### Location
- Compare job opportunities across countries.
- Identify where a particular role has a stronger presence.

### Remote Work
- Compare work-from-home and non-work-from-home opportunities.
- Understand how remote opportunities vary depending on the selected role or other criteria.

### Job Type
- Explore how opportunities are distributed across different employment types.
- See how changing the job type affects the available job postings.

### Job Platforms
- Identify which job platforms contribute the most postings.
- Compare the leading platforms for different job-market segments.

Because the dashboard is interactive, these insights can change significantly depending on the selected **Job Title, Country, and Job Type**.
